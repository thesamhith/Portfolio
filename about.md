    <h2>My approach</h2>
    <p>I enjoy the space between a vague business question and a confident next step. My process is simple: understand the context, investigate the evidence, communicate the result clearly, and stay honest about what the data can—and cannot—say.</p>

    <p>This site is a working record of that process. Each project is intended to show not only the final output, but also the assumptions, trade-offs, and recommendations behind it.</p>

    <h2>What I look for</h2>
